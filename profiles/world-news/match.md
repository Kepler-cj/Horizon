# World news profile

Use this profile for major international news: geopolitics, armed conflict, elections and leadership changes, national policy with cross-border consequences, major economic and monetary decisions, large-scale disasters, public health emergencies, and significant international agreements.

Typical items affect a country or region beyond a single company or product, or involve named state actors, institutions, or large populations.

Do not use this profile for product releases, technology-specific coverage, routine market moves, sports, entertainment, celebrity coverage, or single-company business news.
